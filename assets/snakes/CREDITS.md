# Snake sprite credits

The snake spritesheets in this folder are from the **PixelSnakes (Free)** asset
pack by **Carysaurus**.

- Files: `Snake{Albino,Blue,Brown,Corn,Green,Red}-Walk.png`
- Format: 224x32 px, seven 32x32 frames of a single slither cycle, facing left.

The pack ships without a licence file. Before distributing a build that
includes these sprites, confirm the terms on the original pack page and record
them here.
